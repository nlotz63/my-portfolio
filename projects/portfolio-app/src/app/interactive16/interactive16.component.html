Overview

  • The yield curve illustrates the relationship between yield to maturity and the time to maturity for debt securities.
  • The curve typically slopes upward, indicating that longer-term debt securities have higher yields than shorter-term ones.
  • The yield curve can be used as a predictor of future interest rate changes and economic activity.
